About us

RDCOMMX is a network installation subcontractor working on aerial and underground builds, splicing and testing for broadband projects across the United States.

What we do

  • Fiber splicing

    Fusion splicing for backbone, distribution and drop cables.

  • Aerial & buried cabling

    Strand, lashing, directional bore and trench placement.

  • OTDR testing

    Loss and OTDR testing with full documentation on every link.

  • FTTx OSP & ISP

    Outside and inside plant construction for fiber-to-the-home builds.

  • HFC projects

    Hybrid fiber-coax node splits, upgrades and maintenance.

  • MDU projects

    Apartment and multi-dwelling building wiring and activation.
